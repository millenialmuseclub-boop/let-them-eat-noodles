## Phase 2 (this pass): +35 dishes, 51 total

Added, by region: **China/HK** (+6: Chow Mein, Zhajiangmian, Hot Dry Noodles, Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles, Scallion Oil Noodles, Wonton Noodles), **Vietnam** (+6: Phở Gà, Bún Bò Huế, Bún Thịt Nướng, Cao Lầu, Mì Quảng, Hủ Tiếu), **Thailand** (+4: Pad See Ew, Pad Kee Mao, Boat Noodles, Rad Na), **Philippines** (+5: Pancit Canton, Pancit Palabok, Pancit Malabon, Sotanghon, Batchoy), **Korea** (+4: Bibim Guksu, Janchi Guksu, Kalguksu, Kongguksu), **Japan non-ramen** (+5: Kitsune Udon, Curry Udon, Kake Soba, Somen, Hiyashi Chuka), **Malaysia/Singapore** (+5: Char Kway Teow, Hokkien Mee, Mee Goreng, Assam Laksa, Mee Rebus).

11 new noodle types were added to support these (Cantonese Egg Noodle, Thick Rice Vermicelli, Cao Lầu Noodle, Mì Quảng Noodle, Hủ Tiếu Noodle, Sen Yai, Mung Bean Vermicelli, Miki Noodle, Somen, Kalguksu Noodle, Yellow Egg Noodle) — several deliberately reuse existing types to prove the relationship model further (see `NOODLE_DOMAIN_MODEL.md`).

Judgment calls made against the candidate list: Sukhothai Noodles, Khanom Chin, Pancit Miki, Pancit Habhab, Pancit Lomi, Jjolmyeon, Bun Rieu, Curry Mee, Bak Chor Mee, Mie Aceh, Liangpi, Ants Climbing a Tree, and full Indonesian/Myanmar-beyond-Mohinga depth were evaluated and deliberately **not** added this pass — not because they're uninteresting, but to keep the pass bounded and every added record genuinely complete, per the master spec's explicit instruction to use judgment rather than mechanically force every candidate in.

## Deliberately not seeded yet

South Asia (no strong noodle-tradition candidate identified), Indonesia beyond what Malay-Peninsula dishes already touch, Cambodia, Laos, Taiwan, and deeper Myanmar coverage beyond Mohinga — genuine future candidates, not force-fit to hit a round number. Japanese ramen — out of scope per the Ramen boundary.

## Phase 3: remaining candidates toward the family's ~60-dish ceiling

Ohn No Khao Swe / Shan Noodles (Myanmar), Mie Goreng / Mie Ayam / Soto Mie (Indonesia), Jjolmyeon / additional Korean regional noodles, Sukhothai Noodles / Khanom Chin (Thailand), Bak Chor Mee / Curry Mee (Singapore), Pancit Miki / Pancit Habhab / Pancit Lomi (Philippines depth), Zhajiangmian's Korean cousin jjajangmyeon as its own dish (currently only cross-referenced, not modeled), Liangpi and other Xi'an-region cold noodle dishes.

Each must meet the same completeness bar as Phase 1/2 (see `NOODLE_DOMAIN_MODEL.md`) before being counted — no shallow records.
